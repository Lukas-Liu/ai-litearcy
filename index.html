AI Literacy Atlas

Every AI literacy framework answers why it matters, who should learn it, what should be learned, and how to teach it, from a particular context and set of values. Pooled together, the frameworks form a many-sided space. Choosing your own assumptions collapses that space onto one concrete context, and what falls inside becomes your framework.
